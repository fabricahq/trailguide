The Trailguide mark is a folded map with two rule lines in its center panel.
It brings wayfinding and written guidance together: skills and rules that help an agent find its way.
Use these assets when you link to Trail Guide, write about it, or build an integration.

## Logos

Use dark ink on light backgrounds and light ink on dark backgrounds. Prefer SVG for sharp rendering at any display density.
The wordmarks are outlined, so they need no installed font.
Show the horizontal logo at 160px wide or larger; below that, use the symbol.

## Colors

The identity uses one flat color at a time on a grayscale palette.

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | <code class="whitespace-nowrap">#1C1C1C</code> | The dark logo and symbol |
| Light ink | <code class="whitespace-nowrap">#F2F2F2</code> | The logo on dark backgrounds |
| Surface | <code class="whitespace-nowrap">#F6F6F6</code> | Light panels behind the dark logo |

## Typography

The wordmark is set in Inter Semibold with tight letter spacing.
The downloadable logos contain vector outlines, so they render identically without the font.
The wordmark reads "trailguide" in lowercase. In running text, use your own typeface; do not recreate the wordmark.

## Usage guidelines

- Keep the symbol upright and proportional. Preserve the three map panels, rounded strokes, and two parallel rule lines.
- Keep at least two stroke widths of clear space around the symbol. The exported logos already include padding.
- Use one flat color. Avoid gradients, shadows, outlines, and text inside the symbol.
- Use light ink on dark backgrounds and dark ink on light backgrounds.
- Use the horizontal logo at 160px wide or larger and the symbol below that.
- Favicons and app icons are reserved for the Trail Guide site and its own accounts, which is why they are not offered here. Do not use the symbol as the icon of another site or app.
- Do not combine the symbol with another product's mark or use it to suggest endorsement.
