## Shared brief

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original logo SYMBOL for Fabrica, an umbrella collection of modular software tools for technical teams who assemble and tinker with their own software factories.
This is a new visual direction for the same brand.
Modern, innovative tech identity with an interesting distinctive silhouette; sophisticated and memorable rather than generic corporate.
One solid BLACK symbol on a genuinely transparent alpha background.
Pure flat vector-like shapes, no texture, no outline, no shadows, gradients or perspective.
No words, letters, monograms, arrows, chevrons, healthcare crosses, gears, cubes, generic AI sparkles, nodes or literal diagrams.
One symbol only, centered on a square canvas occupying 80 percent of the canvas in its longest dimension.
Generous clear negative space inside, no fine detail, must work as a tiny 16px header icon.
No checkerboard or backdrop in the output pixels.


## Phase

Direction PHASE / PRECISION: spare, unusual, confident.
A compact tilted lens silhouette constructed from just TWO precisely related curved solid forms.
Think an exact elliptical cut, two offset surfaces brought into alignment, light passing through a carefully shaped opening.
Tension between a smooth continuous outer curve and a single crisp straight interruption.
The two unequal forms balance around an intentional open negative-space lens, not a tiny hole.
Near-circular overall proportions, controlled asymmetry.
Quiet mathematical beauty, exact but not sterile.
Broad substantial shapes rather than thin rings.
No orbiting satellite, planet, eye, camera shutter, aperture, chain, infinity symbol, swoosh or generic ring.
This should feel like a refined emblem for a design-conscious technical tools maker, and NOT a folded ribbon or stacked bent bars.

## Spring

Direction SPRING / ENERGY: expressive, fluid, in motion, delight in making things.
A compact abstract elastic ribbon compressed into a lively asymmetric loop with one unexpected twist.
Two or three broad soft-edged curved masses, generous open gaps, a sense of stored spring energy and playful controlled movement.
An ownable silhouette that feels alive at rest.
Mix generous convex curves with a tight concave bend; energetic but polished and premium.
Give it a slightly off-axis stance, like a shape catching its balance.
NOT a literal coil spring, helix, sphere, globe, infinity sign, chain link, letter S, flame, leaf, running person or AI star.
Avoid the industrial folded metal look and avoid a stack of parallel stripes.
Bold enough to remain readable at tiny size, no wispy tapering to hairlines.
