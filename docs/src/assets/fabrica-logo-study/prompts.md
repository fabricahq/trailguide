# Fabrica industrial logo study

These are temporary concepts generated with the built-in imagegen tool on September 10, 2026.
No mark has user approval.
Each final prompt was the shared brief followed by the corresponding creative direction.

## Shared brief

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original premium abstract logo SYMBOL for Fabrica, an umbrella brand for modular tools that technical teams assemble into local software factories.
The chosen character is INDUSTRIAL: BOLD, COMPACT, ENGINEERED.
Modern innovative software identity, sophisticated graphic design, very strong distinctive silhouette, sturdy proportions, deliberate negative space, readable as a tiny 16px header icon.
Flat solid black on pure white, precise vector-like edges, no shading, no gradients, no 3D, no texture.
One symbol only centered, occupying 80 percent of a square canvas.
No text, no letters, no monogram, no wordmarks, no board or mockup, no extra miniatures.
Avoid F letterforms, arrows, chevrons, rockets, brackets, chains of workflow nodes, interlocking blocks, cubes, medical crosses, shields, gears, AI sparkles, circuitry and generic SaaS logos.

## Die

Creative direction DIE: a dense, custom machined oval or near-circular silhouette with a single decisive off-center negative-space cut suggesting a forming die or an extrusion tool.
Rounded exterior meets an unexpected engineered interior.
Approximately 2-3 large visual elements total, optically balanced but not mirror-symmetrical.
Feel like a small precision component you want to hold, extremely simple and ownable.
No camera shutter, no power button, no letter G, no stock aperture icon.

## Press

Creative direction PRESS: a compact industrial maker's stamp, a bold superellipse silhouette with an ingenious negative-space channel that expresses separate inputs being formed into one piece.
The shape should feel milled and stamped, decisive and original.
Use substantial broad material and one surprising large void.
Avoid a maze, avoid a grid, avoid a square divided into four parts, avoid interlocking brackets.
Keep at most two visual pieces.
A stamp from a future factory.

## Extrude

Creative direction EXTRUDE: two or three heavy parallel strips of material sharing one dramatic smooth bend, forming a tight unified sculptural emblem.
Precision extrusion, rolled steel, software as a material you shape.
Use broad strokes and generous white channels.
A compact nearly square or circular overall silhouette, modest asymmetry and tension, blunt cut terminals, engineered curves.
No flimsy thin lines, no weave crossings, no infinite loop, no fingerprint, no S monogram, no forward arrow.
Bold and restrained.

## Assessment

The generated PNGs have transparent backgrounds.
The temporary component uses their alpha channels as CSS masks so marks inherit the text color.
Die is the strongest candidate in this round.
Press may suggest a laboratory flask, while Extrude may suggest an F despite the shared brief.
All three were visually checked at 16px and 40px in the live docs in light and dark themes.
These are raster explorations, not finished vector assets.
