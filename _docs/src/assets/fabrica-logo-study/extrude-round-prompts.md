## Shared brief

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE new symbol for Fabrica, a collection of modular software tools for technical teams building local software factories.
Input image is a STYLE REFERENCE, the user's favorite concept called Extrude.
Preserve its visual family: industrial, bold, compact, engineered; heavy flat bands, smooth precision bends, blunt cut ends, generous negative spaces, confident modern tech identity.
Create a meaningfully DIFFERENT silhouette, not a tiny variation or a rotation.
A polished professional vector-like logo, solid opaque BLACK mark with crisp clean edges on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background.
Square canvas, mark centered and filling 80% of width and height.
All negative spaces transparent.
NO text, letters, wordmarks, gradients, shadows, texture, shading, 3D, mockups, additional miniatures.
It must remain readable at 16px.
Avoid arrows, chevrons, medical crosses, gears, cubes, generic AI sparkles, literal workflow diagrams.


## Coil

COIL: sculpt a single rolled strip into a compact, asymmetric open loop.
Two broad returning bands describe a near-circular shape around a large offset void.
A short crisp interruption exposes the ends of the material.
Think rolled steel and a precision spring, but rendered with only 2-3 bold planar shapes and absolutely no 3D.
Strong rounded outer silhouette and one surprising engineered cut.
NOT a chain link, infinity sign, letter C, letter G, power icon or loading spinner.
The result should feel related to the reference but rounder, denser, and more self-contained.

## Fold

FOLD: two broad flat ribbons make one tight decisive fold, like heavy sheet metal bent through an oblique corner.
One major curved bend and one sharp clipped junction.
Form a compact asymmetric emblem with a diagonal stance, 2-3 solid pieces, substantial negative space separating overlapping layers.
Neither a literal letter nor a cube or arrow.
Less striped than the reference, fewer pieces, stronger bold silhouette.
The character is engineered strength with a surprising fold.

## Sweep

SWEEP: three bold parallel bands turn together through a single smooth quarter-turn, forming a compact squarish emblem with one softly rounded corner and three precise staggered cut terminals.
Bands have equal substantial weight and generous equal transparent gaps.
Suggest multiple tools moving as one.
Create a continuous sculpted bend rather than the reference's crossing fold.
Graphic tension from a tightly organized fan of machined strips.
No circle surrounding it, no arrowheads, no radiating wifi signal.
Extremely compact and optically balanced.
