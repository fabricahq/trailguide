# READMEs

**Group ID:** `practices/readmes`

**Description:** Write a README that says what the product does for the reader, gets a new user to a working result, states its limits, and links to the full documentation, with a presentation that matches whether the product is primary or secondary.

**When to read this group:** When planning, writing, restructuring, or reviewing a repository's README, such as launching a product, preparing a release that changes setup, or moving material between the README and the documentation.

## How to use this group

5. **Recheck after changes.** When the task’s scope changes, reassess which rules apply. After compaction, reread the rules needed for the current task before continuing.

## Rules

### Give a quick start that runs as written

Rule ID: `fabrica:practices/readmes/give-a-quick-start-that-runs-as-written`

Version: 1.0.0

**When to read:** Before writing, changing, or reviewing install or getting-started instructions in a README, such as install commands, version numbers, setup steps, example commands, and sample output, or before a release that changes any of them.

**Impact:** MEDIUM-HIGH

**Why it matters:** The quick start is the first thing many users run; when it fails or installs an old version, they conclude the product does not work.

**Read full rule:** [Give a quick start that runs as written](../../rules/fabrica/practices/readmes/give-a-quick-start-that-runs-as-written.md)

### Keep the README an entry point, and link to the full documentation

Rule ID: `fabrica:practices/readmes/keep-the-readme-an-entry-point`

Version: 1.0.0

**When to read:** Before adding or reviewing detailed material in a README, such as configuration tables, command references, FAQs, file formats, architecture notes, or build and contribution instructions, or when a README grows well past its quick start.

**Impact:** MEDIUM

**Why it matters:** A README that duplicates the manual drifts out of date with it, and buries the quick start under reference detail most readers do not need yet.

**Read full rule:** [Keep the README an entry point, and link to the full documentation](../../rules/fabrica/practices/readmes/keep-the-readme-an-entry-point.md)

### Match the README's presentation to the product's tier

Rule ID: `fabrica:practices/readmes/match-presentation-to-product-tier`

Version: 1.0.0

**When to read:** Before writing, restructuring, or reviewing a README's overall layout and tone, such as its title block, badges, opening pitch, section headings, and feature descriptions.

**Impact:** MEDIUM

**Why it matters:** A flagship product presented like a manual loses readers before they see its value, and a supporting tool presented like a flagship overstates its importance and hides the facts its readers came for.

**Read full rule:** [Match the README's presentation to the product's tier](../../rules/fabrica/practices/readmes/match-presentation-to-product-tier.md)

### Open with what the product does for the reader

Rule ID: `fabrica:practices/readmes/open-with-what-the-product-does`

Version: 1.0.0

**When to read:** Before writing or reviewing the opening of a README, such as its title, tagline, first paragraphs, badges, and any notices or links placed above the description.

**Impact:** MEDIUM

**Why it matters:** Readers who cannot tell from the first screen what a product would do for them leave or misjudge it, and those who stay follow setup steps without knowing whether the product fits their needs.

**Read full rule:** [Open with what the product does for the reader](../../rules/fabrica/practices/readmes/open-with-what-the-product-does.md)

### State what the product does not do

Rule ID: `fabrica:practices/readmes/state-what-the-product-does-not-do`

Version: 1.0.0

**When to read:** Before writing or reviewing a README's description of what a product can do, such as its features, supported platforms and services, status, or comparisons with alternatives.

**Impact:** MEDIUM

**Why it matters:** Readers who find an unstated limit after adopting a product lose time and trust, and a README that lists only strengths reads as marketing.

**Read full rule:** [State what the product does not do](../../rules/fabrica/practices/readmes/state-what-the-product-does-not-do.md)
